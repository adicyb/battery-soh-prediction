Place NASA PCoE Li-ion battery .mat files here, e.g.:
    B0005.mat
    B0006.mat
    B0007.mat
    B0018.mat

Download from:
    https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/
    (Dataset #5: "Battery Data Set")
or mirror:
    https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/li-ion-battery-aging-datasets

Once a file is placed here, simply run:
    python main.py
It will be detected and used automatically instead of the synthetic fallback data.
